What we collect

Account information: when you sign in with Apple, we receive a unique account identifier from Apple and, if you choose to share it, your email address (which may be an Apple private relay address). We do not receive your Apple password.

Content you create: the places you save and the plans you build, including plan titles, stops and arrival times.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts or photos, and we do not use advertising or analytics trackers.

How we use it

We use this information only to run the app: to keep you signed in and to store and show your saved places and plans.

Third parties

Supabase provides our backend: authentication, database and server functions. Your account information and saved content are stored there.

Apple provides Sign in with Apple and Apple Maps, which displays places and routes in the app. Apple’s own privacy policy applies to your use of those services.

If you use “Add to Calendar” or “Share”, the plan details you choose go to your device’s calendar or to the app you share with. We do not receive them.

Suggest a date

Suggest a date builds a plan using a rules-based scoring method, not artificial intelligence. It compares the vibe ratings we assign to places (such as how easy it is to talk, how intimate or energetic it feels) against the profile of the occasion and flow you choose, and picks the closest matches that are near each other.

This runs on our own curated place data, which is stored in our backend (Supabase). The occasion and flow you pick are not sent to any other service, and no AI or third-party language-model provider is involved. If that changes, we will update this policy before the change takes effect.

Suggestions can be wrong or out of date; please check details before you go.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Saved → Settings → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account, saved places and plans from our systems. It cannot be undone.
